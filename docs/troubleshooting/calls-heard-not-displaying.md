# Incoming Calls are heard but not visible

If you can hear a call coming through but the incoming call screen doesn't appear, your Phone app is likely out of date. Updating the Phone app through the Play Store usually resolves this.

### Solution

1. Open the `Play Store`

2. Search for `Phone`

3. Find `Phone by Google` in the results

4. Press `Update`

:::tip

If the button shows `Open` instead of `Update`, tap on the `Phone by Google` listing to open its page, then check if there's an `Update` button there.

:::

Once the update finishes, incoming calls should display correctly again.
